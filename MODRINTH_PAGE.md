# Copycat Casing

Encase your Create shafts in **any block you like** and make your machines look the way you want.

Right-click a Copycat Encased Shaft with a full block (white wool, stone, planks, glass, ...) and it takes on
that block's look. It still transmits rotation exactly like a normal shaft, and it also takes on the block's
sound, friction and blast resistance. Biome-tinted blocks such as grass and leaves keep their colour.

## Features

- **Copycat Encased Shaft:** a full-block casing around a shaft, with a clean opening where the shaft passes through.
- **Copycat Encased Shaft Slab:** a half-height version, placed like a vanilla slab (bottom or top half).
- Sneak + Wrench to remove the casing and get your block back.
- Breaking the casing, or blowing it up, drops the block it was wearing.

## Crafting

- Shaft + Copycat Panel -> Copycat Encased Shaft
- Copycat Encased Shaft -> 2 slabs, and 2 slabs -> Copycat Encased Shaft

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**, **NeoForge** 21.1.219+
- [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) 6.0.10+

## Limitations

- Blocks with block entities (chests, furnaces) and non-full blocks (stairs, fences) can't be used as a casing.
- No double slab, no waterlogging.
